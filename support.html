Send It Support

Send It is a private job-search tracker: your applications, interviews, contacts, and follow-ups in one place, on your device.

Need help or found a bug?
Email jeff@loboz.com and include what you were doing and what you expected to happen.

Where is my data kept?

On your device, always. If your device is signed into iCloud, Send It also syncs to your private iCloud so a new phone picks up where you left off. Settings shows you plainly whether iCloud sync is set up. There are no accounts and no servers of ours.

Can I get my data out?

Always. Settings has a full export as CSV and JSON files, yours to keep anywhere.

How do I save a job posting?

From Safari or any browser, tap Share on the posting and choose Send It. The role, the link, and the posting text are captured so the record survives even after the posting is taken down.
